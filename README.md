# GFP: General Fitness Performance

**How much better is your whole fitness than three months ago? Not "I feel better", but a number.**

GFP turns your training results into one score. Push-ups, running times and lifted weights all flow into a single number, so progress becomes something you can measure in percent.

## The origin of the idea
I got the idea while reading *Atomic Habits* by James Clear. At the end of the book, basketball players are rated with a single number built from their stats. Every season a player has to improve that number by at least about 1 percent, otherwise they are cut from the team.

That stuck with me. Performance becomes much clearer once it is a number. In fitness this is hard, though. You can say "I did more push-ups", but you cannot easily say how many percent better your overall fitness is. GFP, short for *General Fitness Performance*, is my answer to that.

## The formula is the product
The app is not the real product. The real product is the formula behind the score. It is built on sports research about how different muscle fibers work together and how efficiently they do it, and it aims to be as biologically correct as possible.

My first idea was an Excel table where everything is calculated. The app turned out to be a better way to put the formula into people's hands.

## What the score covers
- **CSP, Calisthenics:** bodyweight strength, from push-ups to advanced skills.
- **EP, Endurance:** running, cycling and other endurance tests.
- **WSP, Weight training:** barbell and dumbbell strength, optional.

The three parts combine into one GFP score, with a rating from beginner to elite.
